# ARI SMART RO v1.0.22 — Corporate Training + Offline Field Work

## Corporate 30-day training
- 30-day bilingual Hindi/English customer-service and field-professionalism programme.
- Each day is planned for 60 minutes: concept, Admin/Trainer demonstration or role-play, employee practice, and feedback.
- Admin can open each employee training assignment as Trainer.
- Daily trainer review records behaviour, communication and knowledge scores (1–5), strengths, gaps, coaching action and notes.
- The employee receives the coaching action so weaknesses can be corrected deliberately.
- Existing videos remain part of the relevant lessons and must be watched completely before completion.
- Existing final quiz, compliance reminders, overdue strike and Admin-reviewed penalty workflow remain.

## Offline field work retained from v1.0.21
- Downloaded jobs remain available without internet.
- Service/complaint/payment/removal status changes can queue locally and sync on the next successful online job refresh.
- GPS coordinates, before/after photos and signatures are retained locally when upload is unavailable and synchronized later.
- Installation details can queue offline; OTP generation/verification and inventory-part validation still require server connectivity.

## Calling desk
- v1.0.20 Caller Command Center remains included with lead creation, customer search, outcomes, priorities, follow-ups, history and KPI dashboard.
