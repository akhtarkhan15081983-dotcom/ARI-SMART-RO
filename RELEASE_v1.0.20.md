# ARI SMART RO v1.0.20 — Caller Command Center

- Callers can create and own new leads from their desk.
- All active existing customers are searchable by name, phone, customer/card number, area or city.
- Calling an existing customer reuses an open lead and never duplicates the customer master record.
- Unified pipeline supports Pending, No Answer, Callback, Interested, Not Interested, Wrong Number and Converted outcomes.
- Priority management: Low, Normal, High and Urgent.
- Every saved call creates a timestamped activity record with caller, notes, outcome, duration and next follow-up.
- Dashboard KPIs show calls today, overdue follow-ups, interested leads and conversions.
- Pipeline, Customers and History are available as dedicated tabs in one Caller Command Center.
- Existing role permissions remain enforced; calling employees cannot access restricted rent-management screens.
