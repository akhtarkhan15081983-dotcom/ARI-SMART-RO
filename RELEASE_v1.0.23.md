# ARI SMART RO v1.0.23 — Training Certification

## ARI Professional Certification

- Adds an ARI Certified Professional certificate to the 30-day corporate training programme.
- Certification requires all training days/lessons completed, final quiz passed, Trainer review completed for every lesson, and Trainer average of at least 3/5.
- Only Admin can issue the certificate after the employee becomes eligible.
- Certificate records a unique certificate number, verification code, quiz score, Trainer average, final weighted score, issue date and one-year validity date.
- Employee Training shows a certification card with status and QR verification.
- QR verification uses a public read-only verification endpoint and reports VALID, EXPIRED or REVOKED.
- Admin can revoke an issued certificate with an audit reason.
- Automated backend tests cover issuance requirements and public verification.

## Existing v1.0.22 features retained

- 30-day Hindi/English corporate training, planned at 60 minutes per day.
- Admin/Trainer role-play coaching and behaviour, communication and knowledge reviews.
- Offline field-work queue for weak/no-network locations.
- Professional Caller Command Center.
